Why ScrollFlow?

⚡ Zero Dependencies

Written in pure ES6 JavaScript. No jQuery, no bloat. Just 2KB gzipped.

🎨 Reveal Animations

Fade up, slide in, zoom in. Trigger animations easily when elements enter the viewport.

🌌 Parallax Engine

Smooth parallax effects for backgrounds and elements with a simple data attribute.

How It Works

Just include the script and use data attributes. No complex configuration required.

1. Installation

<link rel="stylesheet" href="css/scrollflow.css">
<script src="src/scrollflow.js"></script>


2. Reveal Animations

<div data-sf-reveal="fade-up">
    I will fade up when visible!
</div>


3. Parallax Effect

<div data-sf-parallax="0.5">
    I move specifically slower than scroll!
</div>
